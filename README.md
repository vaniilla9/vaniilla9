**A dark-fantasy, turn-based RPG for iPhone, coming soon to the App Store.** It's built natively in SwiftUI, with a
seven-chapter story, tactical battles, 21 classes and a deterministic engine covered by 139 automated tests and a
balance simulator. That's ~19,400 lines of Swift, in English, Spanish and German, with no third-party dependencies.
